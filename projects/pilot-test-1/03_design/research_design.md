# Research Design: pilot-test-1

## Conditions

| Condition | Role | Content summary |
|---|---|---|
| Control | Baseline | "Save $5 today." |
| Treatment | Manipulation | "Save $5 today — for your family's future." |

### Behavioral Principle
The treatment applies **beneficiary framing** (other-regarding / family motive). Grounding an immediate sacrifice ($5 today) in a relational purpose ("for your family's future") shifts focus from short-term opportunity cost and personal deprivation toward emotional duty and long-term protection for dependents.

## Outcome Variable

- **Primary:** `click_sign_up` (Binary: 0 = Did not click / Decline, 1 = Clicked "Sign Up")
- Full specification available in `variables_spec.md`.
