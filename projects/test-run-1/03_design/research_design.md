# Research Design: test-run-1

**Unblinded as of 2026-07-17.** Was run blind (researcher withheld arm assignment until after data collection); now revealed: condition_1 = treatment, condition_2 = control.

## Conditions

| Condition | Role | Content summary |
|---|---|---|
| condition_1 | Treatment | Financial savings app; savings option framed as "$5 a day" |
| condition_2 | Control | Financial savings app; savings option framed as "$150 a month" |

Both conditions share identical app description and question structure; the only manipulated element is the framing of the savings amount ($5/day vs. $150/month -- economically near-equivalent amounts, ~$150/month either way). This is a temporal reframing manipulation: presenting a recurring cost as a small daily amount rather than the equivalent monthly aggregate, hypothesized to increase perceived affordability and uptake.
