# Research Design: test-run-1

**Blinded test.** The researcher has withheld which of the two conditions is the intended control/baseline vs. treatment/manipulation arm until after data collection. Both arms are tracked under neutral labels only.

## Conditions

| Condition | Content summary |
|---|---|
| condition_1 | Financial savings app; savings option framed as "$5 a day" |
| condition_2 | Financial savings app; savings option framed as "$150 a month" |

Both conditions share identical app description and question structure; the only manipulated element is the framing of the savings amount ($5/day vs. $150/month -- economically near-equivalent amounts, ~$150/month either way).

No behavioral-principle annotation is given per condition since the researcher has not disclosed which arm represents which theoretical mechanism.
